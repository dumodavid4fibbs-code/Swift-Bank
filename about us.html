About Us

Built by people who got tired of confusing banking.

SwiftBank started with a simple idea: banking shouldn't feel like a mystery. No hidden fees, no fine print built to confuse you — just an honest place to keep and grow your money.

Our Story

SwiftBank was founded in 2024 by a small team who'd spent years working inside traditional banks and walked away frustrated by how unnecessarily complicated everything was. Opening an account took a branch visit and a stack of paperwork. Understanding a fee meant calling support and waiting on hold.

We built SwiftBank to be the opposite of that: an account you can open in minutes, a dashboard that shows you exactly where your money is, and a team that actually answers the phone. We're still small enough to care about every customer — and that's on purpose.

Our Values

Transparency

Every fee, rate, and term is written in plain language — no fine print designed to confuse you.

Security

Bank-grade encryption and mandatory OTP verification protect every account, every time.

Simplicity

If a feature takes more than a few taps to use, we consider it broken and redesign it.

Community

We're a small team that reads every piece of feedback — and often builds what customers ask for.
